# A paper that tells of a held dungeon (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.20.7, the
# ways to hear of a place). tools/dungeon-walkers.sh paper sets [Dungeons]
# TestSite=Fort Ash, TestSiteState=2, MessengerHours=0; the player far from
# the fort.
# - read: the refugees' notice (the ESP's book A76) added and equipped - the
#   game's book menu opens: "news - the player reads ...A76", "news - told to
#   the player (site 0)", "journal - Fort Ash: stage 20 (heard ...";
# - closed: CloseAllMenus - "the paper ... is closed (the book menu now
#   holds 00000000)".
# Run through tools/dungeon-walkers.sh paper.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
ini Performance Enabled=0
ini SelfCheck ShowPanel=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready set gamehour to 12
console-at ready fw 00038EEE
console-at ready cow Tamriel 5 5
console-at read player.additem {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:A76} 1
console-at read player.equipitem {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:A76}
console-at closed CloseAllMenus

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 14
mark read
wait 5
mark opened
wait 2
mark closed
wait 4
mark end
wait 1
