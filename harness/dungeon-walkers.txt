# A siege's and a walker dungeon's walkers in the game (docs/walkers-spec.md
# 4.20.7, step 5). tools/dungeon-walkers.sh sets for the run: [Dungeons]
# TestSite=Fort Ash, TestSiteState=2 (besieged) or 4 (a walker dungeon),
# AttackWalkersMin/Max=8, WalkersAtOnce=6 and [Zombies] DieOnlyBeheaded=0
# (the console's kill ends a walker).
# - outside (Tamriel -9,19, Fort Ash's door): "the pool to site 0 Fort Ash
#   (besieged | walker dungeon; 8 walkers, 6 at once)", six "walker ... into
#   Fort Ash" - by the door (a siege) or among its spots (a walker dungeon);
#   besieged: the kit too, on the far spots;
# - inside, kill1: the six killed by the console - six "walker ... ended",
#   "2 walkers left", the last two come in by the door;
# - kill2: those two killed - a siege: "defended - now refuge"; a walker
#   dungeon: "taken - now refuge, N people", and the kit moves in.
# tgm and tdetect: nobody fights, the count is the console's; time stands.
# Run through tools/dungeon-walkers.sh siege|taken.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
ini Performance Enabled=0
ini SelfCheck ShowPanel=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready tdetect
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready set gamehour to 12
console-at ready fw 00038EEE
console-at ready cow Tamriel -9 19
console-at inside coc FortAsh01
console-at kill1 "{form:OblivionWalkers.esp:A54}".kill
console-at kill1 "{form:OblivionWalkers.esp:A55}".kill
console-at kill1 "{form:OblivionWalkers.esp:A56}".kill
console-at kill1 "{form:OblivionWalkers.esp:A57}".kill
console-at kill1 "{form:OblivionWalkers.esp:A58}".kill
console-at kill1 "{form:OblivionWalkers.esp:A59}".kill
console-at kill2 "{form:OblivionWalkers.esp:A5A}".kill
console-at kill2 "{form:OblivionWalkers.esp:A5B}".kill

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 16
mark outside
wait 2
mark inside
wait 10
mark kill1
wait 8
mark kill2
wait 10
mark end
wait 1
