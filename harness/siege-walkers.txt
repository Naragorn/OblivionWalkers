# The siege's walkers before the gate, counted off as they end, the siege
# over and the gate unlocked (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.12 step 3, built
# 2026-11-04). The run's plugin INI carries [Siege] TestGate=1 (Anvil Castle
# Gate), WalkersMin=30, WalkersMax=30; Dismemberment=0 here, so every kill
# ends a walker (4.17). The player is moved before the gate (its door
# 00006FBA, 500 units out); killall twice ends what stands there.
# OblivionWalkers.log (read by hand - the runner checks OBVR.log only):
# "the player at Anvil Castle Gate - its walkers come out (30 left, 24 at
# once)", 24 "walker ... out before Anvil Castle Gate", "walker ... ended
# before Anvil Castle Gate - N left", the next 6 out, after the second
# killall "the siege is over", "outer door 00006FBA unlocked - read back:
# locked 0", "the walkers before Anvil Castle Gate back in the pool".
#
# Runs on OBVR's harness, from the OBVR folder:
#   python3 tools/hand-script-run.py ../OblivionWalkers/harness/siege-walkers.txt --headless \
#       --artifact-root ../OblivionWalkers/artifacts/hand-script

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Dismemberment=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready player.moveto 00006FBA 0 -500 0
console-at kill1 killall
console-at kill2 killall

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
wait 1

wait 20

wait 5
mark kill1
wait 10
mark refilled
wait 5
