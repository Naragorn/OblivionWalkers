# A held place besieged by Daedra and defended (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.22a
# step 5c, the reward's words): tools/dungeon-walkers.sh daedrasiege makes
# Toadstool Hollow (in the Linux pool) a refuge besieged by 4 Daedra
# ([Dungeons] TestSite, TestSiteState=2, TestDaedra=1), its dead banished
# after 5 s. The player goes in (coc ToadstoolHollow): the placer brings the
# Daedra (inside only in the first cell); killall twice - the siege is
# beaten, "defended", and the reward says "the Daedra are beaten".

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
ini Performance Enabled=0
ini SelfCheck ShowPanel=0

console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready set gamehour to 12
console-at ready fw 00038EEE
console-at inside coc ToadstoolHollow
console-at kill1 killall
console-at kill2 killall
console-at close CloseAllMenus

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 1

wait 2
