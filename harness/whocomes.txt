# Who comes (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.24): [Walkers] Mode and [Daedra] Mode,
# set by tools/whocomes-harness.sh. The console places two of
# LL0ZombieVariant (00034095, the game's zombies), two of LL0Goblin100
# (00084B2C, goblins only) and a bandit (00069AD1, generic) 300 units
# before the player. The roster at "counted" shows what the spawn
# replacement made of the two lists; killall at "counted" ends them all,
# and the plugin's log says whether the bandit waits to rise.
#
#   Walkers 1 (the game's zombies only): the zombie list gives walkers, the
#     goblins stay goblins, the bandit "stays down (Walkers Mode 1 ...)".
#   Walkers 2 (the apocalypse): both lists give walkers, the bandit "waits
#     to rise".
#   Walkers 0, Daedra 0 (the mod off): the zombies stay the game's zombies,
#     the goblins goblins, the bandit stays down; "the mod is off".
#
# The onboarding runs (whocomes-harness.sh onboard / onboardoff) use the
# same placing; the boxes come some 5 s after the load and are answered by
# [Onboarding] TestAnswers.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready player.setav chameleon 100
console-at ready player.placeatme 00034095 2 300 0
console-at ready player.placeatme 00084B2C 2 300 0
console-at ready player.placeatme 00069AD1 1 300 0

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 10
mark counted
console-at counted killall
wait 40
mark end
wait 1
