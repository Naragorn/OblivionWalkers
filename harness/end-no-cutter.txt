# Without a head cutter every kill ends a walker (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.17;
# Nadi, 2026-10-30: "bei Dismemberment=0 killt jeder schlag und walker steht
# auch nicht auf"). OBVR with Dismemberment=0 sends 'WGDM' 0: no cutter. One
# walker (0x801) placed before the player in god mode, killed by the player,
# then 40 s: OblivionWalkers.log (read by hand - the runner checks OBVR.log
# only) "End: walker ... ended (nobody cuts heads ...)", no "Rise: ... gets up".
#
# Runs on OBVR's harness, from the OBVR folder:
#   python3 tools/hand-script-run.py ../OblivionWalkers/harness/end-no-cutter.txt --headless \
#       --artifact-root ../OblivionWalkers/artifacts/hand-script

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Dismemberment=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:801} 1 200 0
console-at kill {actor}.kill player

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
wait 1
mark ready
wait 12
mark kill
wait 40
mark end
wait 1
