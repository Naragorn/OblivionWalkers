# The news names the nearest held place (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.20.7a A):
# tools/dungeon-newsnear.sh sets [Dungeons] Enabled=1, Journal=1, Rumors=1,
# no TestSite, NewPlacesWithin=0 (the places anywhere), ActiveMin=ActiveMax=8,
# MessengerHours=0 (no runner). After the load the eight places are rolled
# with the player inside (his place not known - the kinds' order); then he is
# taken to cow Tamriel -30 10 (the west) and to cow Tamriel 30 -20 (the
# south-east), time held (timescale 0). At each place the log's "news - the
# site now" names the site with news nearest him; the wrapper checks it
# against the active places (their rolls in the log, their doors in
# src/DungeonCandidates.h), and that the two places' news differ.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
ini Performance Enabled=0
ini SelfCheck ShowPanel=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at west cow Tamriel -30 10
console-at west2 CloseAllMenus
console-at east cow Tamriel 30 -20
console-at east2 CloseAllMenus

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
wait 1
mark ready
wait 6
mark west
wait 8
mark west2
wait 4
mark east
wait 8
mark east2
wait 4
mark end
wait 1
