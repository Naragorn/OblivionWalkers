# Without a head cutter a power attack's kill ends a walker (docs/walkers-spec.md
# 4.17 point 6; Nadi, 2026-10-30: "lass den powerattack test per harness
# laufen, bzw wie machen wir das nun für flat gamer"). OBVR with
# Dismemberment=0 sends 'WGDM' 0: no cutter. One walker (0x801) placed before
# the player in god mode, then:
#   1. killed by the player without a power attack ({actor}.kill player):
#      "End: killer 00000014 power attacking: 0", "down - it gets up again",
#      within 20-30 s "Rise: ... gets up";
#   2. the player's power attack group played (PlayGroup AttackPower 1, a
#      stand-in for the flat player's held attack - no blow lands) and the
#      walker killed by the player while it plays: "power attacking: 1",
#      "ended (a power attack, and no head cutter)", no rise in 40 s.
# What it covers: the killer's IsPowerAttacking asked into the quest variable
# at the death and the verdict. What it does not: a real held attack's blow
# (the anim at the hit's frame) - flat players and a headset test tell that.
# OblivionWalkers.log read by hand (the runner checks OBVR.log only): "End:
# killer 00000014 power attacking: 0", "down - it gets up again", "gets up at",
# "power attacking: 1", "ended (a power attack, and no head cutter)".
#
# Runs on OBVR's harness, from the OBVR folder:
#   python3 tools/hand-script-run.py ../OblivionWalkers/harness/end-power-attack.txt --headless \
#       --artifact-root ../OblivionWalkers/artifacts/hand-script

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Dismemberment=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:801} 1 200 0
console-at kill1 {actor}.kill player
console-at power player.playgroup AttackPower 1
console-at kill2 {actor}.kill player

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
wait 1
mark ready
wait 12
mark kill1
wait 35
mark risen
wait 2
mark power
wait 0.3
mark kill2
wait 40
mark end
wait 1
