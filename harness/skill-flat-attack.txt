# Where a vanilla (flat) attack trains the weapon skill (docs/walkers-spec.md
# 4.3, part C; Nadi, 2026-10-27: "du baust für die flat leute ohne obvr diese
# option in walkers"): OBVR's hands off, a longsword equipped and drawn (F),
# a bandit (00069AD1) placed 80 units in front, its AI off (tai), the player
# attacking with the right trigger (OBVR's controls without hands: the
# trigger is the attack - to be seen here) ten times. OblivionWalkers.log's
# "Skills: the player's skill 0E use ... (from <return address> ...)" lines
# give the call site of the weapon skill and EDI there; the run is a probe,
# the decision comes after. Then the bandit killed, a walker (0x801) placed
# in front, still frozen, and attacked ten times the same way: "the player's
# landed hit on <walker> ... NOT COUNTED (a walker)" and no weapon skill use
# after it, where the bandit's hits read "counted" and a 0E use each. Then
# the AI back on (tai again), the walker put into combat with him while the
# player holds the left trigger 20 s (to be seen: is that the block without
# hands) - "skill 0F ... (from 005F5C0B, a block, the attacker <walker>) -
# HELD BACK".
#
# Runs on OBVR's harness, from the OBVR folder:
#   python3 tools/hand-script-run.py ../OblivionWalkers/harness/skill-flat-attack.txt --headless \
#       --artifact-root ../OblivionWalkers/artifacts/hand-script
