# Fame for walkers cut down (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.8, [Zombies]
# FamePerKills): a walker (the ESP's 0x801) placed at the player and killed
# with the player as its killer (`Kill player`: the console's Kill takes the
# killer as its argument - xOBSE's OnDeath hands it over). With the deployed
# INI at FamePerKills=1 (set by hand for the run, back to 50 after) the
# plugin logs "Fame: walker ... cut down by the player - 0 of 1 toward the
# next point, a point earned" and, at the next tick, "Fame: 1 point for
# walkers cut down (ModPCFame handed to xOBSE: 1)". The control is the
# killall after it: a second walker killed without a killer counts nothing
# (no second "cut down by the player").
#
# Results 2026-10-24 (artifacts/hand-script/fame/):
#   21:01 (20261024-210153) PASS: "killer 00000014" for the first walker,
#   the point earned and "ModPCFame handed to xOBSE: 1"; the second, killed
#   by killall ("killer 00000000"), counted nothing.
#
# Runs on OBVR's harness, from the OBVR folder:
#   python3 tools/hand-script-run.py ../OblivionWalkers/harness/fame.txt --headless \
#       --artifact-root ../OblivionWalkers/artifacts/hand-script

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready player.placeatme WalkersZombie1 1
console-at killed {actor}.kill player
console-at control player.placeatme WalkersZombie2 1
console-at control2 killall

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 4
mark killed
wait 3
mark control
wait 2
mark control2
wait 3
mark end
wait 1
