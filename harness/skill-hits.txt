# Do blows on the player train his armor, and does OnHit come for them?
# (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.3; Nadi, 2026-10-27: "Ja. Auch klären ob VR
# Schläge überhaupt skill üben"). The player wears an iron cuirass (Heavy
# Armor, 0001C6D1), his health set to 5000 (not god mode, which may skip the
# hit), and is attacked first by a bandit (00069AD1, the control: his blows
# train, "trained") and then by a walker (the ESP's 0x801: "HELD BACK"), each
# put into combat with him (startcombat) for 25 s and then killed by the
# console. OblivionWalkers.log (copied into the run's folder by hand): the
# "Hit: ... hit by ..." lines (does xOBSE's OnHit come for them at all) and
# "Skills: the player's skill 12 use ..." lines (does the hook see the armor
# use, and what does it decide).
#
# The walker comes FIRST since 2026-10-27 20:50 (the marks keep their names
# "bandit"/"walker" for the first and second attacker): the log's lines are
# few per session, and the run of 20:47 spent them all on the bandit.
#
# Runs on OBVR's harness, from the OBVR folder:
#   python3 tools/hand-script-run.py ../OblivionWalkers/harness/skill-hits.txt --headless \
#       --artifact-root ../OblivionWalkers/artifacts/hand-script

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
console-at ready player.additem 0001C6D1 1
console-at ready player.equipitem 0001C6D1
console-at ready player.setav health 5000
console-at bandit player.placeatme WalkersZombie1 1 100 0
console-at bandit-placed {actor}.startcombat player
console-at bandit-done {actor}.kill
console-at walker player.placeatme 00069AD1 1 100 0
console-at walker-placed {actor}.startcombat player
console-at walker-done {actor}.kill

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 4
mark bandit
wait 3
mark bandit-placed
wait 25
mark bandit-done
wait 3
mark walker
wait 3
mark walker-placed
wait 25
mark walker-done
wait 3
mark end
