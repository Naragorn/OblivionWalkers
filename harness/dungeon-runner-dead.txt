# A dead runner is replaced (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.20.7).
# tools/dungeon-walkers.sh runner-dead sets two besieged places (TestSite
# Fort Ash and one more: ActiveMin=ActiveMax=2, only WeightBesieged),
# MessengerHours=1, no toll.
# - the first runner comes and speaks (dungeon-runner.txt); closed1: the
#   game's boxes closed; kill: the console kills him; away1: the player far
#   off - "runner - taken away";
# - fast: three seconds at timescale 3000 - two and a half game hours;
# - the second errand: "runner - he was dead: raised and dressed again (1)",
#   "sent with news of kind 2", "told to speak ... his 3D 1", "he told his
#   news" (the second place's quest).
# Run through tools/dungeon-walkers.sh runner-dead.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
ini Performance Enabled=0
ini SelfCheck ShowPanel=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready cow Tamriel 5 5
console-at closed1 CloseAllMenus
console-at kill "{form:OblivionWalkers.esp:A78}".kill
console-at away1 cow Tamriel 20 20
console-at fast set timescale to 3000
console-at slow set timescale to 0
console-at closed2 CloseAllMenus

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 34
mark closed1
wait 2
mark kill
wait 3
mark away1
wait 8
mark fast
wait 3
mark slow
wait 36
mark closed2
wait 3
mark end
wait 1
