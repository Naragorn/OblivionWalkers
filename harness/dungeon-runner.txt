# The runner for help (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.20.7, the ways to hear of a
# place). tools/dungeon-walkers.sh runner sets [Dungeons] TestSite=Fort Ash,
# TestSiteState=2 (besieged), MessengerHours=1; the player stands out of
# doors far from the fort (Tamriel 5,5).
# - "runner - sent with news of kind 2 (MoveTo player ...)", a second on
#   "runner - told to speak to the player (StartConversation ...)";
# - he comes up and speaks (the picture at 'spoken'); his greeting's result
#   script sets WalkersDungeonHeard: "news - told to the player (site 0)",
#   "journal - Fort Ash: stage 20 (heard ...", "runner - he told his news";
# - closed: the game's boxes closed; away: the player far off (Tamriel
#   20,20) - "runner - taken away".
# Run through tools/dungeon-walkers.sh runner.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
ini Performance Enabled=0
ini SelfCheck ShowPanel=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready set gamehour to 12
console-at ready fw 00038EEE
console-at ready cow Tamriel 5 5
console-at closed CloseAllMenus
console-at away cow Tamriel 20 20

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 18
mark spoken
wait 12
mark closed
wait 4
mark away
wait 14
mark end
wait 1
