# Slow aggression, roused and calmed (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.21, 2026-11-11;
# Nadi: "warum dann nicht einfach wieder auf 100 setzen sobald der timer weg
# ist"). A walker (the ESP's 0x801) 600 units in front of the player: after
# its drawn 5..10 s it is roused - "walker ... roused" with its Aggression
# above its record 100 - and fights; the player then steps 4000 units away
# (beyond SlowAggressionUnits 1000, nobody else near): after
# SlowAggressionCalmSeconds (10) "calms down" and its Aggression is back at
# the record's. At Tamriel -29,5 (the middle -116736, 22528; away to
# x -112000). tgm: the player does not die; weather and time fixed; the
# spawn replacement off for the run (tools/slow-aggression-rouse.sh), so the
# walker is alone. Run through tools/slow-aggression-rouse.sh.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
ini Performance Enabled=0
ini SelfCheck ShowPanel=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready set gamehour to 12
console-at ready fw 00038EEE
console-at ready cow Tamriel -29 5
console-at loaded player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:801} 1
console-at placed player.setangle z 0
console-at placed {actor}.moveto player 0 600 0
console-at away player.setpos x -112000
console-at away player.setpos z 2500

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 15
mark loaded
wait 2
mark placed
wait 20
mark away
wait 25
mark end
wait 1
