# Slow aggression, roused and calmed (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.21, 2026-11-11;
# Nadi: "warum dann nicht einfach wieder auf 100 setzen sobald der timer weg
# ist"). A walker (the ESP's 0x801) 600 units in front of the player: after
# its drawn 5..10 s it is roused - "walker ... roused" with its Aggression
# above its record 100 - and fights; the player then goes 3000 units away
# (beyond SlowAggressionUnits 1000; the walker, 17-18 units/s, comes about
# 450 nearer in 25 s): after SlowAggressionCalmSeconds (10) with nobody near
# it "calms down" and its Aggression is back at the record's.
# The place: Tamriel -29,-11 (middle -116736, -43008), no spawn point and no
# placed actor of Oblivion.esm within three cells (artifacts/esm, made by
# tools/esm-spawns.py, 2026-11-12). The first run at -29,5 (2026-11-11) sat
# among a deer herd's spawn points: a deer and two others stayed near the
# walker and one of them killed it - no calm to see. Mods may still put
# someone there: the checker lists every "time near" that is not the player.
# Away: 3000 east, from 4000 high (tgm: the fall does nothing). Weather and
# time fixed; the spawn replacement off for the run
# (tools/slow-aggression-rouse.sh), so the walker is alone. Run through
# tools/slow-aggression-rouse.sh.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
ini Performance Enabled=0
ini SelfCheck ShowPanel=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready set gamehour to 12
console-at ready fw 00038EEE
console-at ready cow Tamriel -29 -11
console-at loaded player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:801} 1
console-at placed player.setangle z 0
console-at placed {actor}.moveto player 0 600 0
console-at away player.setpos x -113736
console-at away player.setpos z 4000

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 15
mark loaded
wait 2
mark placed
wait 20
mark away
wait 25
mark end
