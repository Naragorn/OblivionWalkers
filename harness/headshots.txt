# Spells that end a walker as a lost head does (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.17b).
# DieOnlyBeheaded on (the default). The player casts with OBVR's left hand
# (raised, the trigger - as OBVR's spell-casting-hand.txt), at a walker placed
# 300 units before him and held in place (SetRestrained); the console's Cast
# cannot make the player cast a target spell at an actor (CS wiki Cast:
# "cannot force him to cast Touch or Target range spells at other actors").
# Each case in a cell of its own (Tamriel -28..-26, 5), so {actor} - the
# actor nearest the player - is the walker just placed:
#   a  burning: SEStdFireFrostDam1Novice (0004DC23, 7 fire damage) cast at
#      it, killed 2 s later - "dies burning", stays down
#   b  overkill: StandardFireDamageTarget5Master (000A97E3, 110 fire) cast at
#      it - "a spell took N of its N health at once" (or burning); the bolt's
#      distance to the head bone logged ("Headshot: spell projectile")
#   c  control: killed with no fire - "gets up again"
# Arrows are not here: a placed archer did not shoot the walker in the run of
# 2026-11-14 19:20 (it was killed first).
# The save's companion (the ESP's A78, a camp's escort that follows the
# player, essential) is sent to the Market District for the run, or it is
# the nearest actor.
# The hand pitched up 30 degrees: at 0 the laser points 40 degrees down.
# Run through tools/headshots-harness.sh.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
ini Performance Enabled=0
ini SelfCheck ShowPanel=0
console player.addspell 0004DC23
console player.addspell 000A97E3
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:A78}.positioncell 0 0 0 0 ICMarketDistrict
console-at ready tdetect
console-at ready cow Tamriel -28 5
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready set gamehour to 12
console-at ready fw 00038EEE
console-at loaded SetActiveSpell 0004DC23
console-at loaded player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:801} 1 300 0
console-at a-placed {actor}.setrestrained 1
console-at a-burnt {actor}.kill player
console-at a-done cow Tamriel -27 5
console-at b-there SetActiveSpell 000A97E3
console-at b-there player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:802} 1 300 0
console-at b-placed {actor}.setrestrained 1
console-at b-done cow Tamriel -26 5
console-at c-there player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:803} 1 300 0
console-at c-placed {actor}.kill player

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.25 -0.60 -0.10
left pos -0.15 -0.30 -0.45
left rot 30 0 0
wait 1
mark ready
wait 20
mark loaded
wait 2
mark a-placed
wait 1
left trigger 1
wait 0.6
left trigger 0
wait 1.4
mark a-burnt
wait 4
mark a-done
wait 6
mark b-there
wait 2
mark b-placed
wait 1
left trigger 1
wait 0.6
left trigger 0
wait 4
mark b-done
wait 6
mark c-there
wait 2
mark c-placed
wait 4
mark end
wait 1
