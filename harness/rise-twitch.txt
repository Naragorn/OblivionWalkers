# Risen walkers that lie on the ground and twitch (docs/walkers-spec.md 7a;
# Nadi, 2026-11-10, Windows: "walker manchmal am boden wiederbelebt ...
# zucken aber auch nicht aufstehen"). Four walkers killed four ways, each
# getting up again 20 to 30 s later (DieOnlyBeheaded, OBVR's head cutter
# on, no head cut):
#   a  killed standing
#   b  killed in flight - pushed away hard, killed the frame after
#   c  killed lying - pushed down, killed once it lies
#   d  killed standing, the player far away (its cell unloaded) while it
#      gets up, back after 60 s (to -28, 5: every case's cell loaded again)
# Each case in a cell of its own (Tamriel -29..-26, 5; e at -17, 5), so the harness's
# {actor} - the actor nearest the player - is the one just placed.
#   e  killed by an NPC (Nadi, 2026-11-10: "kill durch npc"): killed with
#      Oblivion.esm's persistent Skingrad jailor (0002C0B9, always held) as
#      the killer - with OthersEndPercent=100 it ends ("felled by someone
#      else") and stays down; it must not be raised
# Every raise is watched (RiseLogic.h StandWatch): one still within 16
# units of its spot 20 s after the raise logs "Risen: ... not up "
# with its health, fatigue, 3D, process, package and flags.
# tools/rise-twitch.sh reads OblivionWalkers.log: each walker's death, its
# raise and whether it walked off.
#
# Runs on OBVR's harness, from the OBVR folder:
#   python3 tools/hand-script-run.py ../OblivionWalkers/harness/rise-twitch.txt --headless \
#       --artifact-root ../OblivionWalkers/artifacts/hand-script

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
ini Performance Enabled=0
ini SelfCheck ShowPanel=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready tdetect
console-at ready cow Tamriel -29 5
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready set gamehour to 12
console-at loaded player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:801} 1
console-at a-placed {actor}.kill player
console-at a-done cow Tamriel -28 5
console-at b-there player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:802} 1
console-at b-placed player.pushactoraway {actor} 20
console-at b-placed {actor}.kill player
console-at b-done cow Tamriel -27 5
console-at c-there player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:803} 1
console-at c-placed player.pushactoraway {actor} 5
console-at c-down {actor}.kill player
console-at c-done cow Tamriel -26 5
console-at d-there player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:804} 1
console-at d-placed {actor}.kill player
console-at d-dead cow Tamriel -17 5
console-at e-there player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:801} 1
console-at e-placed {actor}.kill 0002C0B9
console-at away cow Tamriel -28 5

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 20
mark loaded
wait 2
mark a-placed
wait 3
mark a-done
wait 6
mark b-there
wait 2
mark b-placed
wait 3
mark b-done
wait 6
mark c-there
wait 2
mark c-placed
wait 1.5
mark c-down
wait 3
mark c-done
wait 6
mark d-there
wait 2
mark d-placed
wait 3
mark d-dead
wait 6
mark e-there
wait 2
mark e-placed
wait 4
mark e-done
wait 60
mark away
wait 30
mark t30
wait 30
mark end
wait 1
