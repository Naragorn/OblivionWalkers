# Spawn replacement at the engine's own spawn points (docs/walkers-spec.md
# 4.6): the cell Tamriel -29,5 and its loaded grid hold 16 leveled spawn
# points and no placed actor (tools/esm-spawns.py placed.tsv); spawn-count.txt
# read 30 actors there on 2026-10-23 without the plugin - 18 deer, 6 wolves
# (0006E912), 4 bear cubs (0018AE3A), 2 bandits.
#
# With the replacement the wolves' and the cubs' entries point at the
# walkers: if the cells spawn fresh, the roster at the seen marks reads
# walkers (0x00080x) and no wolf; the deer (harmless animals) and the
# bandits (people) stay. A wolf there means the harness save carried that
# spawn from an earlier visit (spawn-count.txt's caveat) - or the
# replacement missed the list; the plugin's "Spawns:" lines tell which.
#
# Result 2026-10-24 11:17 PASS (artifacts/hand-script/spawn-replace-outdoors/
# 20261024-111725): 39 actors - 31 walkers (11000801 x6, 802 x9, 803 x9,
# 804 x7), 4 deer, a bandit (00069AD3) and two MOO Imperial Legion
# Foresters (NPCs, 0D257C0D/0D257C10); no wolf, no cub. The cells spawned
# fresh, so spawn-count.txt's caveat is settled.
#
# Runs on OBVR's harness, from the OBVR folder:
#   python3 tools/hand-script-run.py ../OblivionWalkers/harness/spawn-replace-outdoors.txt --headless \
#       --artifact-root ../OblivionWalkers/artifacts/hand-script
