# A Daedra siege across a save and a load while its Daedra stand (docs/
# walkers-spec.md 8c: 'PLAC' and the red sky across a save), on
# daedra-siege.txt. The run's plugin INI (tools/daedra-harness.sh siegesave)
# carries the siege's test keys and [Daedra] MarchWeather=1. The player is
# moved before Anvil's Castle Gate (its door 00006FBA, 500 units out): the
# placer brings 4 Daedra and the sky turns red. With them standing the game is
# saved and loaded: the placed Daedra are written ('PLAC'), deleted after the
# load, the siege places its own again, the sky red again. Then killall
# until the siege is over and the sky let go.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Hands Dismemberment=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready player.moveto 00006FBA 0 -500 0
console-at saved save WalkersSiegeSaveTest
console-at reload load WalkersSiegeSaveTest
console-at kill1 killall
console-at kill2 killall
console-at kill3 killall

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
wait 1
mark ready
wait 15
mark out
wait 4
mark saved
wait 6
mark reload
wait 25
mark kill1
wait 14
mark kill2
wait 14
mark kill3
wait 40
mark end
wait 1
