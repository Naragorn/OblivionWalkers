# Walkers walk (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.4, [Zombies] Walk): the bandit is
# placed 900 units north of the player in the hall and killed; with the
# deployed INI at [Rise] AfterSeconds=10, JitterSeconds=0 (set by hand for
# the run, back to the defaults after) he rises as a walker 10 s later and
# comes at the untouchable player across the hall. The plugin logs every two
# seconds "Walk: walker ... flags ..., N units/s, M runs turned to walks" -
# run once with Walk=1 and once with Walk=0 (by hand in the deployed INI):
# the speeds of the two are the measure, and "runs turned" above 0 with
# Walk=1 shows the AI asked to run and got a walk. The pictures at the
# coming marks show the gait.
#
# Results 2026-10-24 (artifacts/hand-script/walker-speed/):
#   Walk=1, 10:26 (20261024-102627): flags 0101 throughout the approach, 31
#   to 65 units/s, 618 runs turned to walks - the AI asked to run on every
#   frame and walked; it reached the player after about 24 s.
#   Walk=0, 10:29 (20261024-102942): mostly 0101 at 25 to 54 units/s (the
#   game's zombie walks most of the way), one stretch at 0201, 196 units/s -
#   a run; it reached the player after about 10 s.
# So walk-only holds the walker to its walking pace, and the vanilla zombie
# does run in bursts without it. (The 10:27 run with Walk=0 logged no speed:
# the measurement was gated on Walk=1 then.)
#
# Runs on OBVR's harness, from the OBVR folder:
#   python3 tools/hand-script-run.py ../OblivionWalkers/harness/walker-speed.txt --headless \
#       --artifact-root ../OblivionWalkers/artifacts/hand-script
