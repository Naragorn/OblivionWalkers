# The risen keep their loot (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.15, Nadi 2026-10-24
# afternoon): with [Zombies] NoLoot=1, the default, a walker risen from a
# corpse stays lootable - the fallen's items went over to it. rise-creature's
# placement and rise: the Redguard bandit killed, 20 to 30 s later up as a
# walker with his items; then killall again and the console activates the
# nearest corpse - the walker's (the man's own corpse is disabled). OBVR's
# line at the opened mark names the container ("Hands: at the mark - menu
# ... (Container)"), and the plugin's log has no "can no longer be
# activated" for it: the swap noted the walker as risen before its first
# kill, so neither that kill nor the second set it destroyed.
#
# Runs on OBVR's harness, from the OBVR folder:
#   python3 tools/hand-script-run.py ../OblivionWalkers/harness/rise-loot.txt --headless \
#       --artifact-root ../OblivionWalkers/artifacts/hand-script

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
console-at ready player.placeatme 00069AD1 1
console-at ready tgm
console-at placed player.setangle z 0
console-at placed {actor}.moveto player 0 150 0
console-at moved killall
console-at risen killall
console-at dead2 {actor}.activate player

expect HandScript: finished
expect 00080
expect (Container)
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 6
mark placed
wait 1
mark moved
wait 3
mark dead
wait 32
mark risen
wait 4
mark dead2
wait 3
mark opened
wait 1
mark end
wait 1
