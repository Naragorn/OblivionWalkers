# A Daedra outpost (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.22a step 5c): tools/dungeon-
# walkers.sh outpost makes Toadstool Hollow (in the pool on the Linux list of 2026-11-16, Fort Ash is not) a walker dungeon held by 4 Daedra
# ([Dungeons] TestSite, TestSiteState=4, TestDaedra=1), its dead banished
# after 5 s. The player goes in (coc ToadstoolHollow, the first cell): the placer
# brings the 4 Daedra by the door and the boss - a Dremora three ranks above
# - deep inside, its boss chest at its feet. killall twice: the Daedra and
# the boss fall, the place is cleared and taken; the dead go back to
# Oblivion, their things in sacks.

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
ini Performance Enabled=0
ini SelfCheck ShowPanel=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready set gamehour to 12
console-at ready fw 00038EEE
console-at inside coc ToadstoolHollow
console-at kill1 killall
console-at kill2 killall
console-at close CloseAllMenus

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
wait 1
mark ready
wait 6
mark inside
wait 16
mark placed
wait 2
mark kill1
wait 10
mark kill2
wait 10
mark close
wait 3
mark end
wait 1
