# Hordes wait out of sight (docs/walkers-spec.md 4.11c): at a gate at most
# [Horde] GateHordesMost groups hold (1), and only while the player is
# outdoors within GateNearUnits (8000) of it; any other group near its gate
# waits just outside the player's loaded grid (LoadedGrid 5).
#
# Two batches of walkers (the ESP's 0x801) near Anvil Main Gate
# (-189812, -27878): three at Tamriel -47,-7 (the cell's middle, ~1400 units
# from the gate) and three at -48,-7 (~4900 units off, ~4100 from the first -
# beyond JoinRadius, so two groups). The first group holds at the gate, the
# second waits ("waits out of the player's grid near Anvil Main Gate"). The
# player then goes to -45,-9 (~10200 units from the gate, two cells over -
# the gate's cells still loaded): the holding group waits too. Back at
# -47,-7, a group moves up to hold ("moves up to hold at Anvil Main Gate").
# tdetect: the walkers do not see the player, so no fight takes them out of
# their packages; weather and time fixed.
#
# Run through tools/horde-gate-wait.sh (checks OblivionWalkers.log).

ini Hands Enabled=1
ini Debug GripTrace=0
ini Performance Enabled=0
ini SelfCheck ShowPanel=0
console-at ready tgm
console-at ready tdetect
console-at ready set timescale to 0
console-at ready set gamehour to 12
console-at ready fw 00038EEE
console-at ready cow Tamriel -48 -7
console-at second player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:801} 3
console-at first cow Tamriel -47 -7
console-at placed player.placeatme {form:OblivionWalkers.esp:801} 3
console-at away cow Tamriel -45 -9
console-at back cow Tamriel -47 -7

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
right pos 0.20 -0.40 -0.30
left pos -0.20 -0.40 -0.30
wait 1
mark ready
wait 15
mark second
wait 3
mark first
wait 15
mark placed
wait 60
mark away
wait 40
mark back
wait 40
mark end
wait 2
